## Brand & Style
The design system embodies organic luxury, clinical mindfulness, and tactile serenity. Engineered specifically for holistic wellness and emotional sanctuary, the visual environment prioritizes psychological decompression over hyper-stimulation.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Organic Minimalism** with soft **Tactile Depth**:
- **Personality:** Contemplative, reassuring, dignified, and whisper-quiet.
- **Target Audience:** Modern professionals, seekers of mindful equilibrium, and intentional users seeking restorative refuge from digital exhaustion.
- **Emotional Response:** An instant exhalation—a transition from sensory overload to restorative calm, reminiscent of linen-draped retreat centers, botanical gardens, and Japanese stone gardens.

## Colors
The palette leverages earth-born botanical tones and gentle contemplative pastels over an unbleached mineral backdrop.

- **Canvas Foundation:** Base canvas utilizes natural warm ivory (`#FAF8F3`), avoiding harsh sterile whites.
- **Primary Surface:** Clean botanical cream (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, layered against soft linen tones (`#F4F1EA`) for recessed tracking modules.
- **Primary Sage (`#7A9A70`, `#8EAA83`):** Evokes healing flora, eucalyptus leaves, and quiet vitality. Used for primary calls-to-action, mindfulness progression rings, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary Lavender (`#C8BFF0`, `#DCD6F5`):** Soft luminous lilac representing intuition and restorative sleep. Deployed for subtle highlights, reflective moments, tags, and ethereal gradient halos.
- **Deep Forest Neutral (`#1B3022`, `#233D2B`):** Deep chlorophyll-infused charcoal providing supreme legibility while mitigating the stark eye-strain of absolute pure black.
- **Structural Borders:** Whisper-weight olive-tinted dividers (`rgba(122, 154, 112, 0.15)`) framing card layouts with delicate structure.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs editorial warmth with ergonomic screen clarity.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in *Playfair Display*, delivering human craft, grace, and an editorial, retreat-like presence. Used for daily reflections, greeting moments, session titles, and quote disclosures.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in *Plus Jakarta Sans*, offering humanist open counters, rounded clarity, and fatigue-free legibility across mobile touch interfaces.
- **Rhythm & Formatting:** Headings should never be forced uppercase; preserve natural sentence case to maintain an intimate, conversational cadence.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid column framework built to feel spacious, meditative, and non-congested.

- **Mobile First Focus:** Designed for natural single-hand ergonomics. Primary thumb zones anchor routine interventions, while contemplation surfaces float cleanly in the upper two-thirds.
- **Canvas Margins:** Outer margins strictly maintain `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile and scale to `1.5rem` (24px) on tablet devices to preserve horizontal breathing room.
- **Vertical Breathing:** Sections employ a deliberate 4-point rhythmic multiplier. Content grouping requires wide vertical offsets (`space-xl`) between emotional sections to allow the user's focus to rest between prompts.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized not through rigid drop shadows, but through luminous botanical layering and soft ambient depth.

- **Surface Tiers:** 
  - Base: `#FAF8F3` (Ground Canvas)
  - Secondary Container: `#F4F1EA` (Recessed interactive wells, search bars, and date strips)
  - Primary Container: `#FFFFFF` (Restorative cards, actionable modals, journaling drawers)
- **Ambient Shadows:** Casts a dual-layer soft ambient veil tinted with deep forest tone: `0px 8px 30px rgba(27, 48, 34, 0.04), 0px 2px 8px rgba(27, 48, 34, 0.02)`. Elements feel buoyant rather than suspended high above the page.
- **Backdrop Blurs:** Navigation bars, bottom audio bars, and mood logging sheets implement subtle frosted blur filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(250, 248, 243, 0.85)`).
- **Outlines:** Low-contrast hairline boundaries (`1px solid rgba(122, 154, 112, 0.12)`) establish spatial clarity without harsh borders.

## Shapes
Forms evoke natural water-smoothed river stones and tactile organic curves.

- **Cards & Modules:** Utilize generous `24px` to `32px` corner radiuses (squircle contours), eliminating sharp tensions across the sensory experience.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills:** Full continuous pill radius (`9999px`) applied to status metrics, category filters, and breathing prompts.
- **Micro Touchpoints:** Minor components (swatches, micro-indicators) feature softly curved radii to uphold the smooth, organic visual grammar.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#7A9A70` with text in `#FAF8F3`. Features a pill shape (`roundedness: 3`), minimum touch target of 52px height, and an ambient sage press state (`#68855F`).
- **Secondary (Reflective):** Soft lavender wash (`rgba(200, 191, 240, 0.35)`) with `#1B3022` text and subtle border.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, underlined or paired with delicate botanical iconography, relying on Deep Forest text (`#1B3022`).

### Chips & Badges
- Continuous pill shape, low-height (28px-34px), featuring a tint-backed surface (`#FAF8F3` or `rgba(200, 191, 240, 0.25)`) with a `1px` subtle boundary (`rgba(122, 154, 112, 0.2)`). Text styled with `label-md` in `#1B3022`.

### Cards & Sanctuary Surfaces
- Encapsulated within 24px-32px rounded squircle containers in `#FFFFFF`.
- Insets of `space-lg` (24px) padding. Framed with `1px solid rgba(122, 154, 112, 0.1)` and the signature ambient shadow.
- Inner elements utilize generous vertical flow to prevent cognitive clutter.

### Input Fields & Reflection Areas
- Continuous rounded-2xl (`20px`) forms filled with `#F4F1EA`.
- Border transitions smoothly from transparent to a warm sage glow (`#7A9A70`) on focus.
- Placeholder text in muted forest slate (`rgba(27, 48, 34, 0.45)`), with primary text in `#1B3022`.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Mood Trackers
- Softly contoured, tactile rounded forms.
- Unchecked: `#FAF8F3` container with a `1.5px` border in `#8EAA83`.
- Checked: Smooth fill in `#7A9A70` with a smooth ivory checkmark. Mood rating radios transform into expressive pebble-like organic avatars.

### Audio & Mindful Session Player
- Persistent bottom docking sheet utilizing frosted warm glass blur (`rgba(250, 248, 243, 0.88)`).
- Progress scrubbers rendered as gentle, 6px organic rounded pill tracks filled with sage green, complete with a tactile pebble play head.
